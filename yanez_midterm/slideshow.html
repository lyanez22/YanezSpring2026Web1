    • why slideshow?

    This animation is called slideshow, where images are in an endless loop going through each image, sliding to the left in order to reveal the following image. The speed is steady and I wanted it to be smooth so users can see each image. The tiles in display are from a Royal Mosque, Isfahan. The inspiration was how these photographs are somewhat grainy and I wanted to do something similar to a film scroll. Historically, these tiles were one of the first discovered. The colors chosen for the text are used on the predominant colors used on these tiles: dark blue, light blue, and yellow.
