5 Inspiring Web Artists

Leo Castañeda https://artport.whitney.org/commissions/camoflux-recall-grotto/

Leo Castañeda is a multimedia artist, exploring the intersection of technology and art through game design. One of Leo's recent web projects is the Camoflux Recall Grotto.

Maya Man https://mayaontheinter.net

Maya Man is a contemporary internet artist exploring identity, femininity, and self-performance online, exhibiting globally and curating projects like HEART. My personal favorite project is: Maya Man A Realistic Day In My Life Living In New York City Oct 29, 2024–Oct 28, 2025

Shakti Mb https://www.shaktimb.com

Shakti Mb, from South India and now in NYC, runs Two Shiny Coins, an experimental studio blending software, video, and web, and holds an MFA from Parsons. I find her work intersting since it intercets with film and web design.

Ramsey Nasser https://nas.sr

Ramsey Nasser is a computer scientist, game designer, and media artist who creates games, tools, and interactive work. I like his pixel artwork and game design.

Steven Jos Phan https://stevenfrom.earth

Steven Jos Phan is a NY-based creative director and technologist with 15 years leading brand, computational, and experiential projects. I find his extensive experience impressive, since he has worked with meta, a24, and Hermes.

Bio

I am an aspiring UX engineer, interested in designing healthcare software, video game interfaces, and graphic design. As a third-year student at Hunter College, double majoring in Human-Computing Interaction and Computer Science, I am learning how to combine design and conducting research in user experience to create prototypes to test and analyze trends that could benefit healthcare applications. I enjoy exploring creative design, such as experimenting with pixel art in video games and 3D modeling. What I enjoy about web production is bridging coding and art together, creating interesting visuals through Adobe Illustrator or Figma and making my ideas come to life through coding.
